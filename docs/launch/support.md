# Support channel and process

## Channel (set before launch)
- Support email: [support address]. Publish it on the help page (set NEXT_PUBLIC_SUPPORT_EMAIL).
- Hours: [e.g. Sunday to Thursday, 10:00 to 18:00 Bangladesh time]. Say these hours on the help page.
- Response target: first reply within 2 working days. Safety issues: as soon as seen.
- Who answers: one named person, with a second person as cover.

## Triage
1. Safety or self-harm mention: follow the crisis steps in the app's guidance and the helpline text on the help page. Never promise confidentiality.
2. Data request (export or delete): verify the person before acting. Use the in-app export and delete flows. Never email personal data in reply.
3. Account locked or two-factor lost: follow docs/runbooks/lost-authenticator.md. Confirm identity through a channel you already trust.
4. Bug: ask for the device, browser, and the time it happened. Do not ask for passwords or codes, ever.
5. Feature request: record it in docs/BACKLOG.md.

## Never do
- Ask for a password, one-time code, or staff secret.
- Share one user's data with another person.
- Change data by hand without writing it in the incident log.

## Records
Every support contact gets a short note: date, category, what was done. Store notes without personal details where possible.
